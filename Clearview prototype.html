Why we ask for things

Why do you need my bank login?

So Clearview can read your balance. We can’t move money or make payments.

Why do you need my IRD number?

Your KiwiSaver provider uses it to report your contributions. We ask once, and show this reason before you type it.

Are there any extra steps or fees?

We show any extra forms or fees before you start an action, not partway through.

Balanced fund allocation

Your KiwiSaver money is split between three types of investment. In a balanced fund that’s mostly shares (growth), with some bonds (fixed interest) and cash to smooth out the ups and downs.
